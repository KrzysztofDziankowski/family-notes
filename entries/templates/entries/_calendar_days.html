{% comment %} The fourteen day boxes shared by the parent and child calendars. Parameters: days, detail_url_name, detail_query, show_groups, and optional id_prefix (keeps heading ids unique when a page renders several calendars). days: exactly fourteen chronological {key, heading, is_weekend, groups}, one per calendar date (key is the ISO date), including dates without entries; is_weekend adds data-weekend to Saturday and Sunday boxes (the weekend heading colour); each day's groups are {key, heading, entries}, one per assignee (children, parents, then "Ogólne"), never empty. An empty day shows "Brak wpisów". With show_groups (the parent calendar) each group gets an h3 and a list whose aria-label ("Jutro, czwartek 8 października, Kasia") repeats the day and the assignee for assistive technology. Without it (the child calendar) the day has one list labelled by its heading (aria-labelledby). Rows hide the type, date and assignee: the headings carry them. detail_query (query string without "?") is appended to every row link. {% endcomment %}
